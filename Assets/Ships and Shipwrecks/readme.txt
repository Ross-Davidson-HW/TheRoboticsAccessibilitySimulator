Thank you for buying our pack!

Contains 2 main models (shipwreck and small ship) and 27 additional models for detailing.
The materials use Unity's new standard shader.

In the folder "Demo" you will find three scenes which show an example of how to use the package.

In the folder "Prefabs" you will find prefabs ready to be dragged into your scene.

In the foldes "Meshes", "Textures" and "Materials" you will find all the corresponding resources used in the prefabs.

To get the look from the screenshots and videos use Unity's standard assets Image Effects: Antialiasing, Vignette and Chromatic Aberration, Depth of Field, Screen Space Ambient Occlusion, Bloom and Tonemapping.
The water from the screenshots and videos is Unity's standard assets Water4simple.
